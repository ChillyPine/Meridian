# Meridian
### Feature List 

#### Dungeons

➤  At {location} - Sends a message when player is at one of the following locations: Simon Says, Under Pillar, At Pad, At EE2, At EE3, In Core, At Mid 

➤  Simon Says Time & Notify Part of Simon Says Time

➤  Custom Dungeon Death Message in Party Chat

➤  Fire Freeze Timer for M3

➤  Box Wither & Blood Keys

➤  See Wither and Blood Keys through Walls (AYOR)

➤  Wither and Blood Key Tracer (AYOR)

➤  Invisible Blood and Wither Doors When Opened

➤  Play Sound on Wither & Blood Key Drop

➤  Play Music on Dungeon End

➤  "The Watcher" Message Hider


### Party Finder

➤ Autokick Players - Configurable to kick players below a certain PB for selectable floors with a toggleable kick message

➤  Party Quick Actions - Adds buttons to kick, ignore, or shitterlist a player when they join your party through party finder

➤  Short Party Finder Queue Message

➤  Show Player Stats in Party Finder - Toggles for class level, catacombs level, total secrets, secret average, S+ PB, 

➤  Show Missing Classes

➤  Shitter List - Toggles for auto-kick shitter (with configurable delay) and announcement to party upon shitter join (with custom message)


### Notifications

➤  Main Toggle for Sending Notifications in All Chat

➤  Main Toggle for Showing Notifications on Screen as Titles

➤  Dungeon Notifications for Bonzo/Spirit/Phoenix used & ready, watcher notifications, blood room progress (pc), dialogue skip, locked chest, skyblock kick

➤  Golden Fish Notification

➤  Show Rag Axe Strength Gained (configurable message, color, and sound)


### ! Commands

➤  Various ! party commands including but not limited to !dt, !coords, !allinv, and !warp

### Mining

➤  Amethyst Crystal Finder


### Miscellaneous

➤  Send Message on Inquisitor

➤  Box Deployables

➤  Custom Etherwarp Sound

➤  Great Spook "Quick Maths" Solver

➤  Trapper ESP with Configurable Radius

➤  Send Vanquisher Coordinates in All Chat and Party Chat

➤  Send Vanquisher Killed Message in All Chat and Party Chat

➤  Receive Waypoints from Chat (similar to soopyv2's and GriffinOwO's)
